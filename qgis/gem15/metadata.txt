about=Adds GEM-15 15 m elevation for the current map view as a multidirectional hillshade, streamed from Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs in Google's public bucket (only the blocks and overview levels on screen are read). Also a live local relief model (LRM, radius presets), a canopy check (where the DTM is a guess under trees), the tiles' edges (where seams may look like scarps), and Processing tools to build VRTs, clip to GeoTIFF, make hillshade (multidirectional, one sun or lit across a fault trend), slope and LRM from a clip, and draw a swath profile across a scarp with a scarp-height estimate.

    Data: GEM-15, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). "This dataset is produced by Google". Please cite Batchu et al. (2026), GEM-15: Global surface and terrain elevation models (15 meters) from sub-meter optical stereo imagery, EarthArXiv preprint, doi:10.31223/X55N75. Heights are EGM96 orthometric (EPSG:5773). The DTM is predicted by machine learning: treat it with care under canopy, and do not map tile seams or acquisition boundaries as scarps.

    An independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Provided as is, without warranty; not for hazard assessment, planning, engineering or safety decisions without lidar and field checks.

    Needs an internet connection; uses the GDAL that ships with QGIS.
